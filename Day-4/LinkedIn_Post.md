WHAT IS A LANGUAGE MODEL?
A language model is an AI system trained to understand, generate, and predict human language. It learns from vast amounts of text data and can answer questions, write stories, translate languages, and even code!

WHY ARE LANGUAGE MODELS IMPORTANT?
Language models are at the heart of modern AI applications. They enable machines to communicate with us, summarize information, and assist in countless tasks. Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4 and Gemini have revolutionized how we interact with technology.

KEY CONCEPTS
• Training Data: Language models learn from huge datasets of books, articles, and web pages.
• Tokens: Text is broken into small pieces (tokens) for processing.
• Context: Modern models use context to generate relevant and coherent responses.
