## Introduction

On Day 3, we explored deep learning and neural networks. Today, we’ll focus on Language Models—AI systems that can understand and generate human language.

## What is a Language Model?
A language model is an AI system trained to predict the next word or sequence of words in a sentence. By learning from massive amounts of text, it can generate coherent and contextually relevant language, answer questions, translate, and more.

## Types of Language Models
- **Statistical Language Models:** Early models based on word probabilities (e.g., n-grams).
- **Neural Language Models:** Use neural networks to capture complex patterns in language.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Deep neural networks trained on huge datasets (e.g., GPT-4, Gemini).

## How Do Language Models Work?
1. **Tokenization:** Text is split into tokens (words or subwords).
2. **Training:** The model learns to predict the next token based on context.
3. **Generation:** The model generates text by predicting one token at a time.

## Real-World Applications
- Chatbots and virtual assistants
- Language translation
- Text summarization
- Code generation
- Content creation

## Why Are Language Models Important?
Language models have transformed how we interact with technology. They enable machines to understand and generate human language, making AI more accessible and useful in everyday life.
