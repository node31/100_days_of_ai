# Day 10: Ethics & Safety in AI (Detailed Blog)

## Introduction

Ethics and safety are critical in AI development. Today, I focused on bias detection—a key challenge in building responsible AI systems. I ran a coding exercise that visualizes how a simple sentiment model can show bias across gender and profession.

## Coding Exercise
The code for today is available in the Day-10 folder as `ai_bias_demo.py`. It trains a sentiment model, predicts on gender/profession pairs, and generates a heatmap image (`ai_bias_heatmap.png`) showing predicted sentiment by gender and profession.

## What Did I Learn?
- AI models can unintentionally reflect or amplify bias present in training data.
- Visualizing predictions helps spot patterns and potential bias.
- Ethics and safety are essential for building responsible AI systems.

## Results
I ran the code and here’s what I found:
- The heatmap image made it easy to see if the model was biased toward any gender or profession.
- Even simple models can show bias, highlighting the need for careful data selection and evaluation.
