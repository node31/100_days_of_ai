DAY 7: NEURAL NETWORKS IN ACTION – HANDWRITTEN DIGIT RECOGNITION | 100 DAYS OF AI

Welcome to Day 7 of my 100 Days of AI journey!

Today, we’re moving from theory to practice by building a real neural network that recognizes handwritten digits (0–9) using the classic digits dataset. This is a real-world example of how neural networks power applications like postal mail sorting, check processing, and digit recognition in forms.

WHAT DID WE BUILD?
We used Python and scikit-learn to train a neural network (MLP) on thousands of handwritten digit images. The model learns to recognize patterns in the pixel data and can accurately predict which digit is shown—even for new, unseen images!

WHY IS THIS IMPORTANT?
Neural networks are the backbone of modern AI, enabling machines to see, read, and understand complex data. This demo shows how a simple neural network can achieve high accuracy on a real-world task.

KEY CONCEPTS
• Neural networks learn from data, just like the human brain.
• Layers and activation functions help the network capture complex patterns.
• Real-world datasets (like handwritten digits) make AI practical and impactful.

TODAY’S REFLECTION
