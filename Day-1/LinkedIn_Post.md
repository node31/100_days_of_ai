WHAT IS AI?
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the science and engineering of making machines think and act like humans. It’s about creating systems that can understand, reason, learn, and interact—sometimes in ways that surprise even their creators! From voice assistants to self-driving cars, AI is already part of our daily lives.


WHY START WITH AI?
AI is transforming every industry: healthcare, finance, education, entertainment, and more. Learning AI today means preparing for the future—whether you want to automate tasks, build smarter apps, or simply understand the technology that’s changing the world.
