## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence. These tasks include:
- Understanding and generating language
- Recognizing images and sounds
- Making decisions and solving problems
- Learning from data and experience

AI is not a single technology, but a collection of methods and approaches that allow computers to mimic aspects of human intelligence.

## Types of AI
- **Narrow AI:** Designed for a specific task (e.g., voice assistants, spam filters)
- **General AI:** Can perform any intellectual task a human can (still theoretical)
- **Superintelligent AI:** Surpasses human intelligence (theoretical and speculative)

Most of the AI we use today is narrow AI, but it’s already having a huge impact on our lives.

## Real-World Examples of AI
- **Virtual Assistants:** Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant
- **Recommendation Systems:** Netflix, Amazon, YouTube
- **Self-Driving Cars:** Tesla Autopilot, Waymo
- **Chatbots:** Customer support bots, language tutors
- **Healthcare:** AI for diagnostics, drug discovery
